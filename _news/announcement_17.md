Singeisen has been awarded a $6,000 CASH Pilot Program grant from the NCCU Office of Research Development for "Recovering North Carolina's Unrecorded Architectural Heritage: An Oral History Initiative." The project will record oral histories with Black architects across North Carolina, train four NCCU students as co-researchers, and establish the founding collection of the North Carolina Black Architects Archive (NCBAA) at NCCU.
