Team Communication Workshop

What's Your Communication Style?

Before we work together as a team, it helps to know how each of us naturally communicates. This quick assessment — about five minutes — sorts your tendencies across four styles: Driver, Influencer, Supporter, and Analyst. There are no better or worse results and nothing is graded; the point is self-awareness, so answer honestly rather than how you think you should come across. When you're done you'll get your style and a short list of how teammates can work with you best — half of team friction is really two communication styles talking past each other, and naming it is the first step to fixing it. Nothing is saved or sent; your results stay on this screen.
