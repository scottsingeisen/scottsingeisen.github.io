Team Thinking Workshop

What's Your Thinking Style?

People don't just communicate differently — they think differently. One teammate wants the big picture, another wants the facts, another wants to try something and see what works. This assessment maps how you approach ideas and problems across five thinking styles: Synthesist, Idealist, Pragmatist, Analyst, and Realist. It takes about ten minutes because you rank each set of answers, so give it some focus. Be honest — there are no right answers, and the ranking only works if it reflects how you actually think. Your result isn't a box you're stuck in; it's a starting point for noticing where you and a teammate see the same problem through very different lenses, and why that's a strength on a team, not a nuisance. Nothing is saved or sent.

How to rank: tap a number for each response. Each number 1–5 is used once per question.
